# Vale Investigations

## Privacy Policy

### Personal data

Vale Investigations does not require an account and does not ask you to provide personal information.

### Data collection and tracking

Vale Investigations does not use advertising, third-party analytics or cross-app tracking.

Vale Investigations does not sell or share personal data.

### Game progress and settings

Game progress, completed investigations and app preferences are stored locally on your device so that you can continue your investigation later.

This information is not sent to us.

### Payments

The current version of Vale Investigations does not contain in-app purchases.

If paid investigations or other purchases are introduced in a future version, payments will be handled through Apple's App Store systems and this privacy policy will be updated where necessary.

### Children

Vale Investigations is not designed to collect personal information from children or adults.

# Exam Ready

## Privacy Policy

### Personal data

Exam Ready does not require an account and does not ask users to provide personal information directly to us.

### App usage

Exam Ready may use anonymous, non-personally identifying usage information to help understand app performance and improve the user experience.

This information is not used to identify individual users or for advertising.

### Local data

App preferences and progress may be stored locally on your device so that the app can remember your settings and activity.

### Purchases

Where Exam Ready offers purchases, transactions are processed by Apple through the App Store.

We do not receive your payment-card details.

### Professional examination content

Exam Ready is an independent revision aid. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by any professional examination body unless expressly stated within the app.
