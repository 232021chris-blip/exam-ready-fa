# Mindset Now – Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 2 October 2026**

Mindset Now is designed with privacy in mind.

## Data Collection

Mindset Now does not collect personal data from users.

The app does not use advertising networks, third-party analytics, tracking technologies or third-party profiling services.

No account or registration is required to use Mindset Now.

## Information Stored on Your Device

Mindset Now may store information you choose to create or save within the app, including:

- favourite phrases
- saved phrases and collections
- personal phrases
- personal thoughts
- app preferences
- theme and mode settings
- notification preferences

This information is used solely to provide the features of the app.

## iCloud

If iCloud is available and enabled on your Apple device, Mindset Now may use Apple's iCloud and CloudKit services to synchronise supported app data between your devices.

Information stored in your private iCloud database is associated with your Apple account and is managed through Apple's services.

The developer does not operate a separate server containing this information and does not use your iCloud data for advertising, analytics or profiling.

## Notifications

Mindset Now can provide optional local notifications containing mindset phrases.

Notification permission is requested through Apple's standard iOS permission system.

You can change or disable notifications at any time through Mindset Now or your device's Settings app.

## Widgets

Mindset Now provides optional Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets.

Widget content is generated from the phrase library included with the app and from the preferences you choose.

## Third-Party Services

Mindset Now does not include third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.

The app uses Apple platform services where required for features such as iCloud, widgets, notifications, Siri, Shortcuts and Focus integration. Apple's handling of information through those services is governed by Apple's own privacy policies.

## Children's Privacy

Mindset Now does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

This privacy policy may be updated if Mindset Now's features or data practices change.

The latest version of this policy will remain available on this page.

## Contact

For privacy or support enquiries relating to Mindset Now, please use the support contact information provided on the Mindset Now App Store listing.
