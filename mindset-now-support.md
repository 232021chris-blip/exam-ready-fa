# Mindset Now – Support

Need help with Mindset Now?

## Using the app

Mindset Now is designed to work without an account.

You can:

- choose a Mode such as Motivation, Work, Calm, Drive or Evening
- choose what you need right now
- save favourite phrases
- keep phrases close
- create collections
- add your own phrases and thoughts
- set daily phrase notifications
- add Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets
- personalise themes and typography

## Notifications

Mindset Now can send optional local notifications.

You can change notification settings inside the app or in:

**iPhone Settings → Notifications → Mindset Now**

## Widgets

To add a Mindset Now widget:

1. Touch and hold an empty area of your Home Screen.
2. Tap **Edit** or the **+** button.
3. Choose **Add Widget**.
4. Search for **Mindset Now**.
5. Choose a widget size and add it.

You can also add supported Mindset Now widgets to the Lock Screen.

## iCloud

Mindset Now can use iCloud to synchronise supported app data between your Apple devices.

Make sure you are signed into iCloud and that iCloud is enabled for Mindset Now.

## Privacy

Mindset Now does not use advertising or third-party analytics and does not require an account.

See the Mindset Now Privacy Policy for more information.
